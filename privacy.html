Healthcoach Local — privacy

This personal installation stores imported health data, original source responses, selected settings and saved answers on its owner’s Mac. Local backups can contain health data. Oura client credentials and tokens are stored in macOS Keychain.

The app retrieves Oura data with the owner’s authorization. When the owner explicitly submits a language-model question and enables sharing, the app sends the question, selected metric catalogue and bounded calculated evidence to OpenAI through the dedicated ChatGPT connection. Structured tag context requires its separate sharing permission. Original archives, GPS routes and free-text notes are excluded from language-model evidence.

The local app has no advertising or public account system. Disconnecting Oura stops local access and removes local tokens while retaining imported history. Oura authorization can also be revoked in the owner’s Oura account. The owner controls local files and backups.

These pages describe this personal installation. This information website is hosted publicly on GitHub Pages. The Healthcoach application runs on the owner’s Mac. GitHub may process visitor information under its own privacy policy: GitHub privacy statement.
